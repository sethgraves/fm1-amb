# DREAM manual

DREAM holds sine tones in just intonation. Switch it on and the drone swells in over a few seconds.

- **PLAY** starts and stops the drone. Tones swell in and fade out slowly.
- **PRESET** picks the piece.
- **K1 PITCH** moves the whole series in semitones, **K2 FINE** in cents. The tones glide together and keep
  their ratios exactly.
- **K3 SPREAD** places the tones across the stereo field.
- **K4 TILT** slopes the levels up the series, in dB per octave.
- **SAVE** keeps everything for the next power-on.

The screen shows each tone's harmonic number, the nearest equal-tempered note, how many cents it sits from it,
and its frequency.
